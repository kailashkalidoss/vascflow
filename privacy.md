## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with the application:

### A. Wellness & Measurement Data
* **Blood Pressure Inputs:** Systolic and diastolic readings manually entered or captured for the right/left arms and right/left ankles (Dorsalis Pedis and Posterior Tibial pulse points).
* **Calculated Metrics:** Calculated ABI ratios, risk category spectrum placements, and historical measurement timestamps.

### B. Camera & Optical Images (Gemini Vision OCR)
* **Temporary Processing:** When you use our camera scanning feature to capture blood pressure display monitors or analog dials, the image is transmitted securely to our AI vision processor (e.g., Google Gemini API) for optical character recognition (OCR).
* **No Image Retention:** Camera feeds and uploaded display photos are processed transiently in memory to extract numerical values and are **never permanently stored** on our servers or used to train public AI models.

### C. Technical & Usage Data
* **Device & Browser Data:** Standard log information, including browser type, operating system, and IP address for system performance and security.
* **Local Storage Data:** If you use local storage on your device, your measurement history resides on your client browser until cleared.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for the following operational purposes:
1. **Delivering Core App Functionality:** Calculating ABI scores, plotting spectrum gauges, displaying trend charts, and generating exportable PDF reports.
2. **AI Optical Extraction:** Processing camera snapshots to automatically populate blood pressure input fields.
3. **Application Security & Improvements:** Monitoring system performance, diagnosing bugs, and preventing unauthorized system access.

## 5. Data Security & Storage Options

* **Local-First Privacy:** By default, your individual blood pressure entries and ABI history are stored locally in your browser’s `localStorage`. You retain total control to clear this data at any time.
* **Encryption Standards:** All web traffic and API transmissions are encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).

## 6. Your Data Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., California CCPA/CPRA, Washington MHMDA, EU GDPR), you have specific rights regarding your personal health and wellness data:
* **Right to Access & Portability:** You can view all logged readings on your dashboard or export them to a PDF report at any time.
* **Right to Erasure (Deletion):** You can delete your calculation history directly within the app settings or clear your browser site data.
* **Opt-Out of Camera Processing:** Use of the AI camera scanner is entirely optional; you can manually input all blood pressure readings without activating camera permissions.
