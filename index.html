Setup · measurement method

How will you measure?

Choose your device. This is an at-home setup: the person being measured should lie supine (flat on their back), well rested and calm.

⚠️ Manual mode: camera / OCR scanning is turned off throughout the app. Read the systolic (top / first Korotkoff sound) value from your dial and type it in.

At-home positioning checklist

  • Person lies supine, legs uncrossed, arms at heart level.
  • Rest 5–10 minutes, calm and quiet — no talking or moving during readings.
  • Use the correct cuff size; arm cuff 1–2 cm above the elbow crease.
  • Ankle cuff on the lower leg just above the malleoli (ankle bones) — no Doppler needed.
  • Take 3 readings per site; the app keeps the highest.
